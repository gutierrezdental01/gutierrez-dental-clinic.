GOOD QUESTIONS

Need the deets?

Here are the things patients ask us most.

Is my appointment confirmed when I fill out the form?

Private Pay bookings are confirmed when the portal successfully reserves a slot. HMO requests require clinic verification before confirmation.

Can I use my HMO?

Contact us with your HMO provider and plan details so we can check current network activation and eligible benefits. Coverage and approvals vary.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Please contact the clinic first to check same-day availability and avoid unnecessary waiting.

What should I bring?

Bring a valid ID and any relevant HMO membership information if you plan to use benefits. Our team can advise on additional requirements.
